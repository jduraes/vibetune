v0.2.7.25 - Fix VGM timing and RC2014 heap clear crash

Major fixes:
- Fixed VGM playback timing to match correct tempo (128 BPM target)
- Removed heap clear operation that caused BAD INT crashes on RC2014
- Implemented proportional VGM timing formula: ((CPU_KHz * 2) / 1024) * 11/16
- Timing now scales correctly across all CPU speeds (Z80, Z180, etc.)

Technical details:
- Heap clear (50KB LDIR) caused interrupt issues on RC2014 7.37MHz Z80
- CP/M already provides clean memory state, making heap clear unnecessary
- VGM timing empirically tuned: vgmfdly=10 for RC2014 gives 128 BPM
- Formula automatically adjusts for faster CPUs (SC126, 20MHz systems, etc.)
- PT3/PT2/MYM playback unaffected and continues to work correctly

Testing:
- Verified on RC2014 @ 7.3728 MHz: penguin.vgm plays at correct tempo
- PT3 files (e.g. rl2wof.pt3) continue to play at correct speed
- No crashes or hangs on RC2014 or SC126

Resolves timing issues with VGM playback being too slow on RC2014.
